The cart

We bring the bar to the wedding, not a box of airpots.

It is the same grinder, the same two-group lever and the same people who work the shop Tuesday morning. The cart is self-contained, so a venue with no plumbing and one outlet is not a problem. Here is what a coordinator usually wants to know before saying yes.

Footprint
7′6″ × 3′2″, 6′4″ tall with the canopy up. Fits through a 36″ door.
Power
One dedicated 20A / 120V circuit within 50 ft. No outlet? Our battery bank runs a full four-hour service, silently.
Water
Fully self-contained: 12 gal fresh in, 12 gal waste out. Nothing goes on your lawn.
Timing
90 min to set up, 45 min to strike. We arrive before your photographer does.
Ground
Level and firm. Grass is fine, deep gravel and stairs need a conversation.
Insured
$2M liability, food handler certified. We send the COI straight to your venue.

How the day runs

T−90

Cart in, canopy up

We find the outlet, level the wheels and dial the grinder to the day's humidity. You will not hear from us.

T−10

Machine at temp

Boiler at pressure, milk cold, cups stacked, signature drink card on the counter.

Cocktail hour

The rush

Two baristas, roughly 65 drinks an hour each. This is when 70% of the orders land.

After dinner

The second wave

Espresso martinis, affogato, decaf for the grandparents. We stay on until your last pour.

T+45

Gone without a trace

Waste water hauled out, floor swept, nothing for your venue to clean up.
